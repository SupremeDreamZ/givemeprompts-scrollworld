The critical path

Less searching. Better information.

The website should behave like the beginning of a knowledgeable sales conversation. It gathers evidence, clarifies urgency, separates verified inventory from sourcing work, and routes the request to a real person.

01 / FAILURE

Start with the situation.

A failed breaker, urgent shutdown, obsolete component, planned replacement, or unidentified part. The visitor does not need perfect terminology to begin.

02 / IDENTIFY

Capture what the equipment can prove.

Catalog number, manufacturer, voltage, amperage, poles, kVA, mounting, label text, and photographs become structured evidence rather than fragments across calls and inboxes.
